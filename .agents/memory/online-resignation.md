---
name: Online resignation behavior
description: The expected leave, result, bot-replacement, and next-match behavior for 509 Yard online games.
---

Leaving an active 1v1 match is a resignation: the opponent receives a win and the quitter receives a loss. In larger multiplayer rooms, a bot takes over the quitter's seat for the current match, then the seat is available to a new player for the next match.

**Why:** the user specified this as the expected behavior for online games.

**How to apply:** use this rule for existing and future online game rooms; do not treat disconnecting or leaving an active match as a neutral exit.
